# Bigwin Digital — Grand Your Brand Day.

A premier full-service digital marketing agency website built for **Bigwin Digital**, specializing in direct-to-consumer (DTC) and e-commerce brand growth.

---

## 🧭 Multi-Page Architecture

1. **Home (`index.html`):**
   * High-impact hero section with "Grand Your Brand Day" branding.
   * Service overview with direct pathways to dedicated service pages.
   * Dynamic E-Commerce ROI Calculator.
   * Documented DTC Case Studies with category filters.
   * 4-Stage Velocity Engine Framework.
   * Tiered Pricing Table (Monthly / Quarterly with 15% discount toggle).
   * Verified Founder Testimonials & FAQs.

2. **Dedicated Service Pages:**
   * **[Website Development & CRO](website-development.html):** Sub-second load speeds, Core Web Vitals optimization, custom Shopify PDPs, slide-out cart drawers with 1-click upsells.
   * **[Search Engine Optimisation](search-engine-optimisation.html):** Transactional keyword mapping, collection architecture, technical schema, and high-tier Digital PR backlinks.
   * **[Social Media Optimisation](social-media-optimisation.html):** Viral short-form video scripting (TikTok & Reels), micro-influencer UGC pipelines, bio funnel routing.
   * **[Performance Marketing](performance-marketing.html):** Meta Ads (CAPI server-side tracking), Google Ads & Performance Max, TikTok Spark Ads scaling to 4.8x+ ROAS.

3. **Company Pages:**
   * **[About Us](about.html):** The Bigwin Digital story, "Grand Your Brand Day" mission, 4 core operating principles, and contrast against legacy agencies.
   * **[Contact Us](contact.html):** Interactive inquiry form with live submit state, direct email/WhatsApp contact info, operating hours, and partner intake capacity indicator.
